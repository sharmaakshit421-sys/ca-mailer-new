Frequently Asked Questions
Is this really free?
Yes! The tool runs entirely in your browser and connects securely via Google's API to your Gmail. Since there are absolutely no servers or databases to maintain on our end, we are offering this 100% free forever for CA and CMA students.
Is my data secure?
Absolutely. Your data never leaves your browser. We don't have backends or databases storing your emails, contacts, or CVs. The Google login happens directly through Google's official pop-up and API, guaranteeing top-tier security.
Can I send emails with attachments?
Yes, you can easily attach a PDF or Word document (such as your CV or Resume) up to 5MB. It will be sent exactly as a standard email attachment directly to all the recipients you added.
Is there a limit on how many emails I can send?
Google restricts personal Gmail accounts to 500 emails per day and Google Workspace accounts to 2000 per day. To ensure your address isn't marked as spam, we enforce taking it slow by adding a 2-5 second delay between emails.
